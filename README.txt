Bring your Honeywell Home thermostat into Homey and control your home's comfort alongside everything else. It works with the Honeywell Home Wi-Fi thermostat range — the T5, T6, T9 and T10. See the indoor temperature and humidity at a glance, set the target temperature, switch between Off, Heat, Cool and Auto, and know at once whether the system is heating, cooling or resting. In Auto mode you can set the heating and cooling setpoints together, and a rolling activity log keeps the recent changes close at hand.

Because it speaks to your thermostat through Honeywell's own service, a "goodnight" or "everyone has left" Flow can adjust the temperature on its own, and changes you make in Homey reach the thermostat right away. It works with accounts from both the Honeywell Home and First Alert apps.

This is an unofficial, community-built app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by Honeywell, Resideo, or First Alert.
